## 2. PLAYLIST CURATION RULES
1. **No Generic "Workout Remix" Tracks:** Avoid unnatural electronic remix covers (e.g. Power Music Workout). Use original high-quality studio tracks.
2. **OutKast / Funk & Groove Vibe:** Sejun responds best to organic, funky, rhythm-heavy tracks like OutKast (`Hey Ya!`), Daft Punk, Dua Lipa, and The Weeknd.
3. **In-Run Skip & Buffer Margin:** Always maintain at least **45~50 minutes** of total playlist duration so mid-run skips or extended cooldowns never result in silent cutoffs.
